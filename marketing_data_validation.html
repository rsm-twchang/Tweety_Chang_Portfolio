Marketing Data Validation

Industry-sponsored graduate team project for a global technology company.

The Problem

Marketing measurement depends on consistent campaign records and comparable spend totals. Manual checks can leave mismatches difficult to trace across source and standardized datasets.

Team Contribution

As part of the MSBA team capstone, I built a Python reconciliation workflow to compare spending, flag discrepancies, and trace their causes. The team also developed campaign-name checks and anomaly flags to support data-quality review.

What We Built

  • Campaign-name validation against configurable lists of accepted components.
  • Spend reconciliation between a Raw Campaign Dataset and a Standardized Reporting Dataset.
  • Anomaly flags to prioritize unusual spending patterns for human review.

How It Works

Load the source datasets, check campaign fields, compare aggregated spending, and export validation flags and reconciliation summaries. Public examples use Campaign A, Campaign B, and Media Platform A as generic labels.

Outcome

The capstone work flagged 900+ discrepancies for investigation. HP leadership adopted the reporting changes I proposed. The team delivered validation tools and exception reports to support review before downstream analysis. An anomaly flag calls for investigation; it does not prove that a record is incorrect.

What I Learned

Reliable analysis starts with checks that reviewers can trace back to their inputs. Configurable rules make validation easier to maintain as reporting conventions change.

Client identifiers, actual campaign names, source filenames, internal metrics, and screenshots are omitted. This summary describes the team workflow at a general level.
